Load Calculator

One Engine. Two Decisions. Endless Applications.

Load Calculator is a variable calculator. It takes multiple various variables that are changeable by quantity, size, weight, and/or time. The operator places the variables needed as input, and the placed variables create the equation. The equation calculates the best output for the outcome requested. Time and timing sit at the core of every calculation the engine runs.

This case places production and shipping variables for manufacturing: streamline the process for greater productivity and output, and less friction.

Processing

What runs, in what order, finishing when. Machine board, run priority, ready-time projections, materials in and recycling out per order and per machine.

Shipping

What goes out, in which box, from which gate, at what time, at what cost. Inventory, gate schedule, load matching, bills of lading, and the watcher.

Processing knows when to react. Shipping knows when not to release. Both decisions come from the same reusable intelligence.
