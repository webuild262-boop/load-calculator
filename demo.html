Load Calculator

One Engine. Two Decisions. Endless Applications.

Load Calculator is a variable calculator. It takes multiple various variables that are changeable by quantity, size, weight, and/or time. The operator places the variables needed as input, and the placed variables create the equation. The equation calculates the best output for the outcome requested. Time and timing sit at the core of every calculation the engine runs. This case places production and shipping variables for manufacturing.

7:00 AM morning fill

Place a variable — you create the equation; it resolves again every time a variable is added or changed

New order — an entry for the matcher
New departure — an entry for the schedule
Thresholds and rates — placed settings

Each entry states what it is, and breaks down by the parameters it carries — quantity, size, weight, and time, and the wider measures a scenario needs: liquid by volume, and chemical as a family (solid, liquid). The demo engine plans an order by its pallets, lane, ready time, and delivery window; the weight, volume, and chemical breakdowns ride on the entry as placed measures, ready for a scenario that runs on them. The alert thresholds are placed values too — 85 and 95 are this demo's, not constants.

Processing — what runs, in what order, finishing when

Production queue — what comes off the machines, in the order it comes off, and when

Delivery — what goes out, in which box, from which dock, at what time, at what cost

Load board — every load a dot; the shade is the calculation talking: green optimal, red needs help
